# P6 6.14 — deadline re-benchmark vs P1 1.1 (issue 6.14 / #309)

Re-measure of signing and slot-processing latency after V4 and island changes,
judged against P1 **1.1**
[`p1-baseline-8e908a5.md`](./p1-baseline-8e908a5.md) (commit `8e908a5`).
Gloas attestation deadline is obtained through the **P4 resolver** (D9), never
a hardcoded bps.

> **This is the Phase 6 NFR gate, not a new baseline.** The yardstick is the
> **three-run median** p99 within **+10 %** of P1 1.1, **same fixture**,
> median-to-median. Do not compare the 200 ms injected A-9 sign path to the
> ~2 ms 1-key in-memory slot-processing column.

**Verdict: PASS** on the two P1 1.1 fixtures (three-run median p99 within +10 %).
Phase exit is **not** blocked on those series.

Gloas **aggregate** (6.9b island root + `sign_aggregate_and_proof_root`) and
**full self-build propose** (6.20 envelope leg) are recorded below as
**ungated / no P1 baseline**. They are not judged against the 230 ms
attestation series. Attestation PASS does **not** cover island/envelope.
